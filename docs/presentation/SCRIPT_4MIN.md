# Lumen Influencer Marketplace — ~4 minute narration (EN)

Today I want to share a thoughtful product idea: **Lumen Influencer Marketplace**.

Brands in Thailand do not struggle to find followers. They struggle to find creators whose recent videos actually fit a product — a Phuket condominium, a Bangkok restaurant, Thai skincare, an island tour, or a fitness membership.

Follower count cannot answer the real question: does this creator’s content, language, audience, and brand-safety profile match what we are selling?

So we designed a marketplace that starts with understanding, not vanity metrics.

Lumen already turns video into structured insight — transcript, topics, language, style, entities, and brand-safety signals. The influencer marketplace sits on top of that intelligence. Brands discover and shortlist creators with explainable match scores. Creators receive briefs, submit drafts, and publish on their own social accounts. Brands review, approve, and record performance.

It is one product story with two consoles: a brand console for discovery and review, and a creator portal for collaboration.

Here is discovery. A Thailand-focused catalog across Bangkok, Phuket, Chiang Mai, Pattaya, and Samui. Teams filter by platform, city, language, and topic, then rank creators against a specific product. Open a profile and see why the score exists — topic relevance, audience and geography, language, content style, engagement quality, posting consistency, brand safety, and commercial fit.

Then collaboration. Invite a creator. They accept, acknowledge the brief, and submit a draft or private review link. The brand approves or requests changes. After approval, the creator records the publication URL. Performance snapshots close the loop — from first outreach to a published post.

How does this integrate with the main Lumen platform? Cleanly, by design. Lumen remains the analysis engine — the system of record for video understanding. The marketplace owns brands, products, campaigns, matching, invitations, briefs, submissions, and delivery workflow. In the live demo today, a mock analysis client uses the same contract as production: submit a video job, check status, receive transcript and topics. Next, we point that client at the real Lumen Analysis API used by the core Lumen product, without rewriting the interface.

That means the marketplace is not a fork of Lumen. It is a product surface that reuses Lumen’s strengths and adds commercial campaign workflow around them.

Phase one discovery and phase two collaboration are already running in demo. Phase three introduces contracts and payments. Phase four scales data sources and learns from campaign outcomes.

Lumen Influencer Marketplace turns video understanding into campaign decisions — thoughtfully, transparently, and ready to plug into the Lumen platform you already know.

Thank you.
