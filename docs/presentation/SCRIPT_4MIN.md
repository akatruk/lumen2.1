# Lumen Influencer Marketplace — ~2 min narration (EN) · 0.4.8 live demo

Today I want to walk through Lumen Influencer Marketplace as it runs live — cyber-glass brand console, real TikTok discovery, live product scan, and honest collaboration UX.

Brands in Thailand do not struggle to find followers. They struggle to find creators whose recent videos actually fit a product — a Bangkok restaurant on Soi Eleven, Thai skincare, a Phuket condo, or an island tour.

Follower count alone cannot answer the real question. So the marketplace starts from the product, not vanity metrics.

On the dashboard, the sidebar shows Core and More — Login sits in the footer. The mode badge reads Live with TikHub and LLM. Two primary actions lead the way: Scan product and Discover TikTok.

Scan a product from a URL, brief, and photo names. OpenRouter builds a Product Resume Card. Decision fields come first — pitch, topics, geography, prohibited claims, and confidence. Name, budget, and tone sit under Details.

Open Discover with that product. Live TikHub returns real creators with non-zero followers. We rank them with explainable reasons — reach, engagement rate, average views, language, and niche. Open a dossier: brand safety stays Pending Analysis until videos are actually scanned — no false Safe badge.

In the creator portal, Act-as lists TikHub creators first. Switch identity and the home subtitle, invitations, and briefs follow that creator — not a stale seed profile.

Lumen remains the analysis engine. The marketplace owns brands, products, discovery, matching, and workflow. TikHub and OpenRouter are live today. Phase three adds contracts and payments.

Lumen Influencer Marketplace turns video understanding into campaign decisions — thoughtfully, transparently, and ready for Thailand F-and-B pilots.
